Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi, or Vinayaka Chavithi, celebrates the arrival of Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. Our Utsav includes puja, decorations, cultural programs, Annadanam and Nimajjanam with everyone in Guntur.
