About Bala Ganesh Youth Housing Board Colony

Bala Ganesh Youth Housing Board Colony, Guntur, comes together every year to celebrate Ganesh Utsav with devotion, unity and enthusiasm. The community celebrates Vinayaka Chavithi, also known as Ganesh Chaturthi, by organizing Ganesh Puja, decorations, cultural programs, Annadanam and Ganesh Nimajjanam activities.

Vinayaka Chavithi is one of the most celebrated festivals in India. It honors Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the patron of arts and sciences.
